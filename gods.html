The Olympians

Greek name, Roman name, and what a child of theirs can do. The gods are not on Mount Olympus — they are scattered across the modern world, and most of them are terrible at parenting.

Note: the Greek and Roman pantheons are the same twelve gods — plus Hades, who usually makes thirteen. The Camp Half-Blood kids and the Camp Jupiter kids are literally cousins — and for a thousand years, neither side knew it.

Big Bads

⏳ Kronos

Leader of the Titans and the villain of the first five books. He was chopped into pieces by his own children and thrown into Tartarus. He wants to eat the world. He is also, awkwardly, Chiron's dad.

🌍 Gaea

The earth goddess, and the force behind the Heroes of Olympus series. Her children the Giants are enormous, and she does not want the Olympians running the show.

👑 The Triumvirate

Nero, Commodus and Caligula — three Roman emperors who split the world into three and tortured the Oracle. Villainy with excellent PR.

⚙️ Akhlys

A wandering goddess of destruction who shows up in a blood-filled chariot drawn by dragons. Pure menace, no motive needed.

🔪 The Furies

Erinyes — the goddesses of vengeance who punish broken oaths. Alecto, Tisiphone and Megaera. They are terrifying and they are completely on the side of justice.

💀 Thanatos

The embodiment of death. He does not kill on his own — he simply cuts life short. He and his sister Hypnos are the reason Nico is "the son of Hades who brings death."

Monsters of the Camp

🐂 Minotaur

The bronze bull-headed monster in the pizza place. Labelled "Monster of the Week."

🕷️ Arachne

A weaver who challenged Athena and was turned into a spider. Currently enormous and very cross.

🦑 Scylla

Six dog heads and a seafood diet. Guards the straits between the two seas.

🐉 Ladon

The hundred-headed dragon that guarded the golden apple until Heracles took it. Gave Luke his scar.

👻 Gorgons

Stheno, Euryale and Medusa. Turn you to stone if you look at them. Medusa's head is now on Athena's shield.

🗼 Talaria

Winged sandals that make you fly. Hermes's legacy, and a very common birthday gift.

🌫️ The Mist

The haze that hides gods and monsters from mortal eyes. Sometimes it thins, and then things get very bad.

🐱 Kat

A sharp-toothed monster of the week or two, with unusual dining habits and a taste for demigods.

Gods From Other Mythologies

Because Riordan's universe does not stop at Olympus.

🇪🇬 Egyptian

Ra, Isis, Osiris, Anubis, Horus, Thoth, Sekhmet, Bes — and the great magicians Carter and Sadie Kane, in The Kane Chronicles. Some of the most dangerous gods in the whole multiverse live in Egypt.

🇳🇴 Norse

Odin, Thor, Frey, Freyja, Loki, Heimdall and Nidhogg — in Magnus Chase and the Gods of Asgard, where the sword Sumarbrander is trying to destroy the world.

🌌 Other

Hypnos, the Dream God, who goes by the Underworld Bank of Sleep. Nix, primordial. Iris, messenger of the gods, who delivers messages that ruin people's days.
