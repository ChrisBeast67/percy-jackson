Why You'll Love It

⚡ Myth you actually know

You've heard of the Minotaur, the Labyrinth, Medusa and the Golden Fleece. Riordan uses all of it — and then makes it modern, dangerous and funny.

😄 Percy's mouth

A sarcastic, ADHD, dyslexic narrator who does not take kindly to gods or monsters. The books read like the hero is telling you the story himself.

🤝 Found family

Percy, Annabeth and Grover against the world. Then the Seven. Then Apollo and Meg. The friendships are the point.

🏛️ A working mythology

Greek and Roman are the same gods with different accents and temperaments — and that mismatch causes real problems when the two camps meet.

📖 Proper consequences

People die and stay dead. Gods get humbled. Characters carry scars and grief from book to book. It does not reset.

🌍 Three mythologies

Greek, Egyptian and Norse, all in one universe, all crossing over. It goes wider than you expect.

About Rick Riordan

Rick Riordan used to be a middle-school social studies teacher, and he is still very much a teacher at heart. The idea for Percy Jackson came from bedtime stories he told his son Haley, who had been diagnosed with ADHD and dyslexia — so Riordan made the hero hyperactive and dyslexic too.

He presented the first manuscript to a group of sixth, seventh and eighth graders for feedback, and with their help came up with the title and the way Percy's sword works. The rest is over 190 million copies of history.
