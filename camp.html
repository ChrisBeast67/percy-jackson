Long Island, New York

Camp Half-Blood

Long Island, New York — 600 acres of training ground for the children of the Olympians, hidden behind a magical barrier so mortals never see it.

The Camp

Run by Chiron, the wise centaur, for three thousand years. Twelve cabins, one arena, one fire that never goes out.

🛡️ The Arena

A massive amphitheatre with shifting walls and ceilings of whatever the gods decide. Where demigods train, where monsters are summoned for practice, and where the Keres show up to collect the losers.

🔥 The Campfire

The heart of camp. Fresh marshmallows, toasted by the fire, and a myth recounted every night by the firelight. Nobody skips the stories — sitting out is a punishment.

🌊 The Lake

Where the canoe race happens, where Kronos's army was defeated, and where Percy has faced down more than one war. Also where the Camp Half-Blood branch of the "Yule Log" tradition happens.

🗡️ The Big House

Chiron's home, the war council chamber, and the place with the best strawberry cake in the entire world. Also where the Camp Half-Blood sheep-balloon festival happens.

🍽️ The Mess Tent

Pegasus wings, ambrosia, and the absolute worst "important mission" meals you will ever eat. Cake and hot chocolate are technically limited to campers only.

📜 The Apple Fall

One golden apple falls from the sky every day. It grants one wish, but it comes with a curse. Kronos wants it badly enough to lead an army for it.

The Cabins

Twelve cabins for the twelve Olympians — plus cabins for Hades, Iris and the other minor gods. Bunking is permanent — the gods decide where you sleep, and they are not open to negotiation.

⚡

Athena

Wisdom, strategy and warfare. Blue-eyed children of Athena are naturally gifted at planning, and the cabin is a rolling war council.

🌊

Poseidon

The cabins of the sea. Percy, Percy's friends, and half the children of Atlantis can control horses and water. Cabin three, where Percy Jackson is the only permanent occupant.

🔥

Ares

War, blood and courage. The Ares cabin has the highest death rate of any cabin — and the bravest reputation. Their games are brutal by design.

🔱

Hades

The cabin that doesn't exist (officially). Nico di Angelo and Hazel Levesque sleep there, alone, in a corner of camp. The undying can see it; everyone else cannot.

🌿

Demeter

Harvest, hunger and the earth. Meg McCaffrey and her siblings. Cabin four, and the only cabin with a garden that grows food.

🏹

Apollo

Archery, music and prophecy. The most cabin of cabins — you are almost guaranteed to make at least one friend there.

⚡

Zeus & Hera

Cabin one and cabin two — the two Olympian thrones. Zeus's cabin is the biggest in camp, and it stands empty, because the King of the Gods has no demigod children. Hera's cabin is the second biggest, and it is empty too.

🍯

Aphrodite

Beauty and charm — dangerous powers, whether you want them or not. The cabin is famously the least combat-focused.

🔨

Hephaestus

Fire, forges and invention. Where Festus the mechanical dragon was built, and where Leo Valdez keeps his poker games.

🌈

Iris

Rainbows, light and the sky. Iris is the goddess of the rainbow and the messenger of the gods. Her cabin is one of the newest at camp — built when Percy asked for cabins for the minor gods too.

🐍

Hermes

Travel, thieves and speed. The cabin is always open, always busy, and always smells of gyros. Luke Castellan was a Hermes cabin hero.

🐐

Artemis

The hunt, the wild, and the moon. A small cabin with fierce hunters. If you are here, your life is very outdoorsy.

Camp Jupiter

The Roman counterpart, hidden in the heart of San Francisco. Where Camp Half-Blood's Greek siblings meet their Roman cousins.

🏛️ Where is it?

Under the golden hills of the Presidio, in the old brick-and-stone building that used to be the Legion headquarters. Run by Fury — a centaur like Chiron, but Roman and far less patient. Roman camp, Roman rules, Roman gods.

🔺 Why it matters

Greek and Roman demigods are the children of the same gods under different names — and the two camps had been separated for a thousand years without ever knowing the other existed. Camp Jupiter is where Jason Grace, Hazel, Frank, Leo and Reyna end up.

Camp Rules

  • Curfew. Everyone must be in their cabin by nine o'clock. Wander late and Chiron drags you back yourself.
  • No fighting. Inside the arena you may fight monsters. Everywhere else, you take it to the arena or you answer to Chiron.
  • Clean the mess tent. Your own dishes, your own mess. Every single day.
  • No leaving camp. Not without permission. The Mist and monsters are both out there.
  • Respect the cabins. You do not enter another cabin without permission, and you certainly do not mess with another god's kids.
  • Two personal quests. Campers must complete two quests chosen by their godly parent before they can go on their third.
  • Cabin inspections. Cabins are inspected on a schedule and they had better be spotless.
  • No swimming in the lake without a chaperone. Especially if you are a child of Poseidon, or a new camper, or both.

Before Percy

Camp existed for three thousand years before Percy Jackson showed up — and the new series tells that story.

🌱 The Wild Zone (2026)

The very first Camp Half-Blood novel. Set in the autumn right after The Lightning Thief, before Percy and friends arrive, told from the point of view of Katie Kim, daughter of Ares. Four new demigods, one autumn, one camp that does not yet know what's coming.

📖 A long history

Chiron has run camp for over three thousand years. Long ago the huntress Kyra brought Jason and Thalia to camp; years later, a very different-looking crew of new campers arrived the same way.

🗓️ The Oracle's Quest

The Oracle of Delphi is a mortal girl — Rachel Elizabeth Dare — who speaks Apollo's prophecies. In The Hidden Oracle, Apollo himself joins camp as a mortal and leads the quest to secure the oracles, so that no one else can control fate.
