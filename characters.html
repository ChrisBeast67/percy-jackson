Demigods, monsters and mortals

Characters

The heroes of Camp Half-Blood — plus the very mortal friends who keep showing up.

The Big Three

⚡ Percy Jackson

Son of Poseidon and the mortal Sally Jackson. Black hair, sea-green eyes, and the ability to control water, horses and ships, breathe underwater, and talk to almost anything that swims. Struggles with ADHD and dyslexia, which is exactly why Riordan wrote him that way.

His sword, Riptide (Greek: Anaklusmos), is a pen that turns into a celestial bronze sword. It always comes back to his pocket. Out of the five main books, three are narrated by him.

🦉 Annabeth Chase

Daughter of Athena and West Point history professor Frederick Chase. Honey-blonde hair, gray eyes, and a brain that never stops. She is a master strategist and a fierce fighter with her enchanted shield, Aegis — and a runner.

She has arachnophobia (fear of spiders) and a family tree that reaches into the Kane Chronicles and Norse books. Architecture and songbirds are her two loves.

🐐 Grover Underwood

Percy's best friend — a satyr with curly reddish-brown hair, goat legs and horns he keeps under his cap. His horns grow as he ages, and he is terrified of being turned into a goat by Pan.

Also a Pan's Labyrinth quest veteran, a leader, and the one who believes in Percy even when nobody else does. And yes, he has a crush on Annabeth for most of the series.

Camp Half-Blood

🔱 Nico di Angelo

Son of Hades and the Italian mortal Maria di Angelo. Italian, black hair, dark eyes, and completely fearless. He summons ghosts with his chess pieces, and he can travel through shadows.

Nico is the first demigod to be claimed by his godly parent, and he helped bring Hazel back from the dead. His boyfriend is Will Solace.

🏹 Will Solace

Son of Apollo, healer of Camp Half-Blood, and the other half of the Nico/Will pairing. He can heal almost anything with his medicine and music — and his laughter can break curses.

🔮 Rachel Elizabeth Dare

The Oracle of Delphi — a mortal girl who can see through the Mist and hear the gods. She delivers prophecies in rhyme, which makes them very hard to follow.

⚔️ Clarisse La Rue

Daughter of Ares and the former head counselor of the Ares cabin. Hot-tempered, courageous, and a brilliant fighter with a deadly electric spear.

👁️ Zoë Nightshade

Daughter of Atlas and Pleione, and a former Hesperid — she is over 2,000 years old, which she finds embarrassing. Artemis's huntress, immortal, and funny.

🔥 Jason Grace

Son of Jupiter and the mortal Beryl Grace. Raised by a wolf goddess named Lupa. He can summon lightning, control the wind and fly. Leader of the Seven.

🗡️ Thalia Grace

Jason's sister, also a child of Jupiter. Turns into a pine tree when she is stressed, which happens a lot. She gave up her immortality to save Luke, and became a star.

👑 Luke Castellan

Son of Hermes. Handsome, blonde, scarred across the face, and able to open any lock just by thinking about it. A hero of Camp, and the driving force of the first series.

🧟 The Pale Man

The Night Huntsman. Robes of darkness, a sickle that can kill in his hands, and the ability to travel by shadow. Percy meets him in the Sea of Monsters — and survives.

👁️ The Oracle

The spirit of Delphi, who wears a robe of darkness over her mortal face. She gives prophecies in rhyme, is imprisoned in a jar beneath the world, and was freed by the Seven.

🐻 Tyson

Percy's half-brother — a Cyclops son of Poseidon. Super strong, immune to fire, with one huge eye and an unnervingly good memory for everyone's worst moment. Sweetest guy at camp.

🏛️ Chiron

The wise centaur who runs Camp Half-Blood. Son of Kronos, tutor of Achilles and many other heroes, and the mentor of every camper. He has been teaching for three thousand years.

The Seven

The prophecy of seven demigods who must unite to save the world. Mostly fulfilled, as it turned out.

⚡ Percy Jackson

Poseidon. The leader, the one who says "kinda" instead of "yeah", and the one who always gets thrown into the worst situation.

🦉 Annabeth Chase

Athena. The strategist. She built the "unconventional" battle plan against Kronos and it worked.

🔱 Nico di Angelo

Hades. The shadow-traveler and the one who went into Tartarus alone more times than anyone should.

👑 Reyna Ramírez-Arellano

Daughter of Bellona and older sister of Queen Hylla of the Amazons. A Puerto Rican demigod with a jaguar-spirit, who took on the literal Minotaur in a pizza place.

🔥 Leo Valdez

Son of Hephaestus. Funny, loyal, and he builds machines out of scrap — including the mechanical dragon Festus.

🪵 Hazel Levesque

Daughter of Pluto, born in the 1940s in New Orleans with a French-quarter mum. She can control precious metals — and she was dead for a very long time.

🔥 Frank Zhang

Son of Mars, Chinese-Canadian, and cousin of Hazel. He can control fire, survive explosions, and is one of the most genuinely decent people in the series.

The Trials of Apollo Cast

☀️ Apollo / Lester Papadopoulos

God of the sun, archery, music and prophecy. Stripped of his powers by Zeus and dropped into a dumpster in Manhattan as a sixteen-year-old mortal named Lester Papadopoulos. Hilarious, vain, and eventually very, very good.

🌾 Meg McCaffrey

Daughter of Demeter, adopted and raised by the Emperor Nero after her father was murdered — and gaslit into blaming herself for his crimes. The bravest person in the book, honestly.

🦢 Hemithea

A demigod goose-swan from the Island of Ogygia who helps Apollo. She wears a toga and has strong opinions about modern plumbing.

👑 The Triumvirate

Three Roman emperors — Nero, Commodus and Caligula — who took over the Oracle of Delphi and split the world into three pieces. The Big Three villains of the series.

From the Related Series

🪄 Carter Kane

Brother of Sadie, son of the god Osiris, and a magician descended from two pharaohs. He can summon fire, control birds and read hieroglyphics.

🪄 Sadie Kane

Carter's sister, daughter of the goddess Isis. She is louder, braver and frankly a bit terrifying with a crook and a cat named Bast.

⚔️ Magnus Chase

Son of the Norse god Frey, homeless Bostonian, and cousin of Annabeth. He carries the sword Sumarbrander, which is trying to kill him, and his family is Greek, Norse and Egyptian all at once.

🐦 Hearthstone

An elf and Magnus's best friend. Part of Samirah's team, and the one who keeps the group from getting themselves killed — usually.

Mortals & Family

💙 Sally Jackson

Percy's mum, who never stops believing in him, punches a school bully, and is the reason Percy is still decent.

📘 Frederick Chase

Annabeth's dad — a history professor at West Point who does not know what his daughter really is, and Annabeth's father, who does, is Poseidon.

🎭 Tristan McLean

Piper's dad, a famous movie star. He knows exactly who he is and just cannot say it out loud.

🕯️ Grover's mum

Grover's mother, a satyr who ran away from her own kind to raise her son human. Everything Grover is brave about comes from her.
